01 / Building now · PayFlex

          
Earned pay. Before payday.

          
PayFlex helps companies offer earned wage access and employee benefits. Employees can access wages they have already earned and transfer available funds to their bank account, before the regular payday.

          
Explore PayFlex↗Product website · Uruguay

        

        

          

            
01
Offered through employers.
Employees of partner companies access the benefit through PayFlex.

            
02
PayFlex advances the funds.
The employee requests an available amount and transfers it to their bank account.

            
03
Employer liquidity stays intact.
PayFlex provides the advance; it is deducted from the employee's pay at month-end.
